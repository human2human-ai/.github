## What we do

People learn when they explain their thinking out loud and someone asks the next question. Online courses rarely allow this, because each learner would need a facilitator. We give every learner one. Teachers design the activity once, then read every conversation and see what the class understood.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**H2H Reflect**<br>
One learner talks through an oral exam, a role play or a reflection, by voice or text, when they are ready.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**H2H Connect**<br>
Small groups meet on camera to debate or work through a case. The facilitator brings in whoever has gone quiet.

</td>
</tr>

## Who we are

We are the team behind [Edunext](https://www.edunext.co), which has run online learning for universities, governments and training providers since 2013. We build AI that leaves teachers in charge and keeps learners talking to each other. We never use learners' conversations to train foundation models.
